EoP Privacy Policy

Effective: August 25, 2026 · Last updated: August 25, 2026

minbitstudio ("we") respects the privacy of users of EoP (Echoes of the Party, the "App"). This policy explains how the App and integrated third-party services process information.

1. Personal data we collect directly

The App does not provide account registration, login, or a proprietary server service. We do not ask users to enter personal data such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers in the App, and we do not transmit such data to our own servers.

2. Information stored on the device

  • Game data: Progress, saves, companions, quests, records, hub state, and App settings are stored on the user's device.
  • Notification information: If permitted, game-related notification schedules and preferences are stored on the device. The App does not use a remote push notification server.
  • Advertising consent: Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may store advertising privacy choices on the device.

Depending on device and operating-system settings, App data may be included in Google backup or Apple iCloud device backup. Those backups are controlled by the user's Google or Apple account settings and the respective provider's privacy policy.

3. Information advertising services may process

The App uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide rewarded ads. Depending on the user's region, consent choices, device settings, and ad configuration, Google and advertising technology providers may automatically process the following information.

IP address and approximate location inferred from itAd delivery, security, and fraud prevention

App launches, taps, ad impressions, clicks, and video viewsAd measurement, analytics, and service improvement

For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Personalized ads and consent controls

Where consent is required by applicable law, the App may display an advertising privacy message through Google UMP at launch. Users can revisit or change their choices under Settings → Ad Privacy Settings. Advertising privacy and advertising ID controls may also be available in the device settings.

5. Third-party and international processing

We do not store personal data on our own servers for sale. During ad delivery, however, Google LLC and the advertising technology providers listed in the UMP message may process information on servers outside the user's country, including in the United States. Data is transmitted over encrypted network connections when the App uses advertising or consent services. Purposes and retention periods follow each provider's policy and the user's consent choices.

  • Advertising and consent: Google LLC Privacy Policy
  • Android system backup: Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple device backup: Apple Privacy Policy

Users may decline advertising consent or disable device backups. This may limit personalized advertising or restoration after changing devices, but the App's core game features remain available.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Game data remains on the device until the user selects Settings → Delete App Save Data, clears the App's data, or uninstalls the App.
  • Cloud backup copies may remain according to Google or Apple policies until deleted through the user's account backup settings.
  • Retention and deletion of information processed by advertising services follow the policies of Google and the applicable advertising technology providers.

7. User rights and choices

Users can change advertising choices and delete local game data in the App settings. Notification permission, advertising privacy, and system backup can also be managed in Android or iOS settings. Privacy inquiries and requests may be submitted using the email address below.

8. Children's privacy

The App does not directly collect a user's date of birth or age and does not provide an account feature that asks children to enter a name, contact information, or similar personal data. Parents and guardians can manage advertising privacy, content restrictions, and backup settings on the device.

9. Security

Game data is stored primarily on the user's device. Advertising and consent services use encrypted transport such as HTTPS. We work to review the App and its third-party SDKs and apply security updates when needed.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when App features, SDKs, or applicable laws change. Material changes will be communicated through App update notices or this page, and the last-updated date will be shown above.
